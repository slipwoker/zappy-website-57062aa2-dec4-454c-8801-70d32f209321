    Discover our work

    Welcome to Regina Sela Design

    We combine artificial intelligence with fashion artistry to create stunning stage costumes. Our specialty is designing costumes for dancers and troupes who perform on stages or participate in dance competitions worldwide.

    Every costume we create is the result of a unique combination of artistic vision, innovative technology, and high professionalism. We believe that a costume is not just a garment - it's an integral part of the performance, telling a story and enhancing movement.

    Our Services

    Dance Competition Costumes

    Unique design of dance competition costumes that combines aesthetics, functionality, and free movement. Each costume is personally tailored to the dance style and competition requirements.

    Professional Troupe Costumes

    Creating complete collections for dance troupes that combine uniformity and unity. We design costumes that highlight the troupe's synergy and enhance group performance.

    AI-Powered Design

    Using advanced artificial intelligence technology to create innovative design ideas. We integrate AI tools to explore unlimited design possibilities and bring unique ideas to life.

    Personal Design Consultation

    Comprehensive consultation meetings to understand your artistic vision. We work in close collaboration with dancers and troupe managers to ensure every costume perfectly reflects your style and message.

    Behind the Scenes: The Creative Process

    Our process begins with an in-depth conversation about the artistic vision and technical requirements. We use artificial intelligence to create dozens of design variations, examine color combinations, and explore possibilities that were never before considered.

    Next, we refine the selected designs, integrate our human expertise and adapt every detail for movement and performance. The result is a costume that combines technological innovation with classical artistry.
